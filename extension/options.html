Math Logic

Math & logic notation, typed straight into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other text box.

How to use it

1 · BackslashType \in, \forall, \RR… pick with Tab, or press Space after an exact name.
2 · Templates\frac, \sumto, \intab insert ⬚ slots. Use Tab to jump between them. Sizes go in the name: \mat2x3, \vec5, \cases3.
3 · Just type-> <= != x^2 a_n turn into → ≤ ≠ x² aₙ.
4 · Plain English/math sum of 1/n^2 from n=1 to infinity then Enter. Or select text and press Alt+M.

AI conversion for /math

Optional. The built-in converter handles common phrasing offline, for free. It's the default and never sends anything anywhere. Turn this on to have an AI handle phrases the offline converter can't understand.

What gets sent, and where
  • Only the phrase you're converting: the text after /math, or the text you selected before pressing Alt+M. Nothing else you type, and no page content, is ever sent.
  • Only when needed: if the offline converter can't understand the phrase, or on every conversion if you tick "Always use AI" below.
  • Sent straight from your browser to the provider you choose, using your own API key. Math Logic has no server of its own. The provider's privacy policy applies to that text.
  • Your key stays on this computer. It's stored only in this browser's local extension storage and isn't synced to your Google account.
